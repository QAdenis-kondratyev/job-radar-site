Privacy Policy

Job Radar is a single-user personal tool. It requests read-only Gmail access (gmail.readonly) only to the owner's dedicated job-alerts mailbox.

Email content is used solely to extract job postings for the owner. Data is stored locally on the owner's machine, is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and is not used for advertising.

Job Radar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
